## Resume Semantics

- **`session_id` is preserved across `codex resume <id>`**: both generations report `01a1578d-d1da-7bf3-bfa7-9d189aad60ce`. Resume does not mint a new session id.
- **`turn_id` is new for the resumed turn** (`01a1578e-37dd…`), so turn correlation remains per-turn and does not collide with the pre-exit turn.
- **`SessionStart(source="resume")`** arrives 52 ms after the post-resume prompt's Enter, at first-turn start; `UserPromptSubmit` follows 56 ms later (108 ms after Enter). As with startup, it is not dispatched while the resumed composer is idle.
- **`SessionEnd` on each clean exit**: gen 1 at 04:24:48.736 (41 ms after `/quit` Enter) and gen 2 at 04:25:07.646 (43 ms after `/quit` Enter).
- The resume pane replays the prior turn's prompt and `OK` answer immediately (visible in `pane-recording-resume/s000002`), confirming history restoration. The composer is ready at 04:24:57.479, 7.7 s after the resume launch.

## KQ Verdicts

- **KQ-2:** `SessionStart` source discriminates `startup` and `resume`; the payload shape is identical otherwise.
- **KQ-3:** Resume keeps `session_id`, resets the turn timeline with a new `turn_id`, and emits one `SessionStart(resume)` + one `SessionEnd` per generation. A reducer can treat `(session_id, turn_id)` as stable across resume and use `SessionStart.source` to detect the generation boundary.
